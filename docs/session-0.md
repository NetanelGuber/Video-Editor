## Real footage

All 30 files in `B:\Videos` were independently probed with pinned FFprobe 9.0.2. All are 1920 × 1080 with `yuv420p` (8-bit components): 27 HEVC and 3 H.264 files, 28 at declared 144/1 fps and 2 at 60/1 fps. Containers include MP4 and MKV. Audio includes AAC, FLAC, and ALAC; several files contain two audio streams. No 4K, 10-bit, or rotation metadata was found. VFR was examined on the selected samples rather than assumed from the declared rates of all 30 files.

The small test set references six originals. Every audio stream below is 48 kHz stereo. Rotation metadata is absent in all six. Each has constant video packet presentation intervals across the full file, allowing container time-base rounding; decoded frame timestamps in the first five seconds agree. Packet timing inspection is not a whole-file decode/corruption check.
